## Opportunity data handoff
Each proposed record should include, where available:
- Opportunity title and provider.
- Official programme URL and official application URL.
- Category and location/delivery mode.
- Applicant nationality and residence rules (separate fields).
- Education/field/level/age/experience/language/document criteria.
- Deadline, timezone, programme dates and whether rolling.
- Funding components individually (do not collapse all support into one vague label).
- Source evidence and date checked.
- Explicit `unknown` for missing facts; never infer.

Research members collect and cross-check. Admin-authorized users perform the platform's formal review/publish action.
